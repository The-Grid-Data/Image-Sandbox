How to Use Image Sandbox

Getting Started

1. Drag and drop an image onto the upload area, or click to browse your files.

2. Pick a Quick Preset (Make White, Make Black) or click "Custom Color" to choose your own.

3. Click a color swatch to select which color in the image you want to change.

4. Use the color picker next to "Replace with" to choose the new color.

5. Click another swatch to change more colors — previous changes are kept. A ✓ marks each changed color.

Managing Color Changes

You can change multiple colors in one session. Each swatch you edit gets a ✓ and a split preview showing original → new color.

Undo — click the Undo button below the swatches (or press Ctrl+Z) to step back through your color changes one at a time.

Remove one — hover over a changed swatch and click the × to revert just that color.

Reset All — clears every color change and starts fresh.

Click a selected swatch again to deselect it without changing anything.

Color Tolerance

The tolerance slider controls how many similar shades get changed. Low = exact match only, high = includes nearby colors. Start low and increase until you get the result you want.

Background Removal

Toggle "Remove background" to erase the background color from your image. The tool works from the edges inward, so it won't accidentally remove colors inside the logo.

Threshold controls how much gets removed. Edge protection adds a safety margin around the logo.

Click Auto to automatically detect the background color from the corners of the image.

Brush Touchup

After changing colors or removing the background, use the brush tools to fix specific areas:

Restore — paint over areas to bring back the original look.

Erase — paint to remove more background (only available when background removal is on).

Use Ctrl+Z to undo brush strokes.

Selective Mode

Click the Selective button to protect part of the image from changes. Everything to the left of the slider stays untouched. Drag the slider to choose where processing stops.

Great for logos that have icons you don't want to change.

Upscale

Toggle "Enhance & upscale" to make the image bigger and sharper. Choose 2x (double size) or 4x (quadruple). 4x takes longer but gives you a much larger image.

Comparison Slider

Drag the vertical line in the preview to compare before and after. Use the Compare dropdown to switch between different views (color only, background only, upscale comparison).

Zoom & Pan

Scroll wheel to zoom in/out. Hold Space + drag to pan around. Press Fit to reset the view.

Download

Click the Download button to save your image. Photos are saved as high-quality PNG. SVG files stay as SVG. If Selective mode is on, the download includes both the protected and processed areas.
